CricScore logo concepts

Each mark is drawn on a 32-unit grid on the scoreboard-green tile, so at 16 px every edge lands on a pixel. Rows show 16, 24, 32, 48 and 96 px, then the one-colour version, then the lockup at 24, 32 and 56 px. Files live in branding/<concept>/.

A. Stumps shipped

Three stumps and a split bail. The most cricket-specific shape, and the crispest at favicon size.

B. Seam

A red ball side-on with its seam in chalk. Familiar, but the seam softens at 16 px.

C. C-ball

A chalk C holding the red ball, echoing the wordmark's dot. Clean, but less obviously cricket.
